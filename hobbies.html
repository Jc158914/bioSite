Running

Juan developed an interest in running after attending a half marathon where his son and daughter participated. Seeing runners of different ages inspired him to challenge himself and become more involved in running.

He enjoys the opportunity to meet new people, stay physically active, and work toward personal goals. Running has become an important part of his routine.

Soccer Officiating Photo

Soccer Officiating

Soccer has been an important part of Juan's life for many years. After coaching his son's soccer team, he began officiating games in 2013.

Officiating allows him to stay involved in a sport he enjoys while interacting with players, coaches, and other officials. He also values the opportunity to help younger players learn and develop.

Family and Friends Photo

Family, Friends, and Food

When Juan is not running or officiating soccer games, he enjoys spending time with family and friends.

Some of his favorite activities include grilling, relaxing by the pool, and enjoying good food and conversation. These moments allow him to relax and spend quality time with the people he loves.

Always Staying Active

Whether he is running, officiating soccer games, or spending time with family and friends, Juan enjoys making the most of his time. His hobbies reflect his outgoing personality, dedication, and appreciation for the people and activities that bring him happiness.
